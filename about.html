THE DEV DECOR DIFFERENCE

Bespoke Joinery Over Factory-Made Compromises

Many modern interior firms sell mass-produced modular boxes with standard dimensions that leave awkward gaps and waste valuable square footage in Indian homes. At DEV DECOR, every single cabinet carcass, drawer unit, and wardrobe shutter is measured millimeter-by-millimeter and custom-built in our Bharuch workshop.

We believe great interior design is not about superficial glitz; it is the silent satisfaction of a soft-close drawer that glides effortlessly after ten years, or a pooja mandir whose sanctum radiates tranquility every morning.

OUR CORE FOUNDATIONS

Built Upon Integrity, Craft and Transparency

Every project undertaken by DEV DECOR in Bharuch is guided by three non-negotiable principles.

01

Precision Woodcraft

We use high-grade calibrated plywood, solid hardwoods like Teak and Sheesham, and high-pressure decorative laminates that resist moisture, termite attacks, and daily wear.

02

Transparent Estimates

No hidden fees or unexpected mid-project billing surprises. We outline raw material brands, hardware specifications, and timelines upfront before commencing work.

03

Direct Craftsman Ownership

You communicate directly with the masters creating your furniture — not commissioned sales agents. This guarantees your custom preferences are executed accurately.
